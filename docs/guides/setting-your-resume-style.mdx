title: "Setting your resume style"
description: "Choose how tailored resumes are written: length, tone, bullets, skills and section order, with presets for each direction you apply in and examples you like."
---

**Settings → Resume style** controls how resumes built from your profile are written. It changes wording and layout only; your experience, dates and results always come from **Career → Knowledge → Profile**.

## Presets

A preset is a set of writing choices for one career direction. Everyone starts with **General**. To add one, choose a starting point (Communications, Digital Marketing, Project Management, Web Development or Other) and select **Add preset**, then adjust it:

- **Picked for posting titles containing**: words that choose this preset automatically, such as "communications" or "project". Words match the start of a title's words, so "project" also matches "Projects".
- **Voice**: tone, bullet style (action then result; challenge, action, result; or one tight line), information density, and regional conventions. Canada is the default: Canadian spelling, and no photo, date of birth, age, marital status or social insurance number.
- **Length**: page targets for experienced and early-career resumes, the usual number of positions, bullets for major and other roles, and words per bullet. **Older roles** can be kept brief.
- **Summary and headline**: whether to write them, and how long the summary can be.
- **Skills**: how many to list, how many groups, and group names to prefer.
- **Section order**: use each selection's recommendation, or always use your order.
- **Anything else, in your words**: notes such as "No first person".

These are guidelines. Nothing you include is cut, and text is never shrunk to fit a page.

Select **Make default** for the preset to use when no keyword matches, and **Save** when you're done.

## Examples you like

Add up to six resumes you think are well written, from your own resumes or as an uploaded PDF, Word or text file, and note what you like about each. In a preset, choose up to three examples to write like. Examples guide voice and bullet shape only: their content is never copied, and a bullet that borrows a phrase from an example is replaced with your own wording.

## When you build a resume

In **Build the resume**, **Style** shows the preset that matches the posting; choose another if you prefer. After assembling, the summary line names the style used.
